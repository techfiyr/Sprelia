How Sprelia helps you practice

Real conversations

Speak out loud with an AI tutor in real time, at a pace that suits you.

Practical scenarios

Everyday life, work, study, travel and social situations, from beginner (A1) to advanced (C1) levels.

Feedback after you speak

Your tutor keeps the conversation natural. Corrections and suggestions come after the session, not in the middle of it.

Track your progress

Set a daily practice target of 5, 10 or 15 minutes and keep a streak going.
