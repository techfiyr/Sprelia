A preview of how practice lines look in the app. Tap a line to replay it. In the real lesson you say each one out loud.

A lesson takes about two minutes

  1. Meet

    Say hello to your tutor and see what you will practise today.

  2. Say it

    Read a short phrase out loud, with a big smile.

  3. Choose

    Pick the natural reply. A wrong answer is fine: try again.

  4. Build

    Put the words in order to make your own sentence.

Feedback after you speak, never in the middle

Your tutor keeps the conversation natural. When it ends you get corrections and phrases to remember, saved in your Mistake Vault.
