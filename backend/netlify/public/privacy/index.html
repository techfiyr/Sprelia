AI processing

Sprelia's tutors are powered by Google's Gemini models. During a voice session your speech is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate replies, and after the session the transcript is analyzed to produce feedback. See the AI disclaimer.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore): account sign-in and storage of your profile and learning data. Firestore data is stored in the Singapore region (asia-southeast1).
  • Google Gemini API: AI conversation and feedback.
  • Netlify: hosting of this website and the Sprelia API. API requests are processed in Netlify's default region in the United States.
  • RevenueCat (planned, with Premium): subscription status.

  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (planned): app usage and crash reports, without transcripts or audio.
