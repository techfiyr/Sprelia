Your tutors are AI

Emma, Alex and Sofia are AI tutors powered by Google's Gemini models. They are not real people, teachers or examiners, and their names, personalities and voices are part of the practice experience.

AI can be wrong

  • Replies, corrections and suggestions can be inaccurate, incomplete or unnatural.
  • Speech recognition can mishear you. To avoid false corrections, uncertain corrections are left out of your feedback.
  • Hermione does not score pronunciation.
  • Levels (A1 to C1) are self-reported and guide practice. They are not an official assessment or certificate.

Feedback comes after the conversation

Tutors keep the conversation natural and do not stop you mid-sentence to correct grammar. Feedback is prepared after the session ends.

Not professional advice

Practice scenarios such as job interviews, meetings or travel problems are role-play. Nothing a tutor says is professional, legal, medical, financial, immigration or career advice, and practice does not guarantee any exam, interview or job outcome.

Be careful what you share

Your speech is processed by our AI provider. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information, such as passwords, financial details, health information or other people's personal data, during practice. See the Privacy Policy.
