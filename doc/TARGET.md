People understand English, but struggle to speak confidently in real situations.

They may know grammar, vocabulary and sentence structures, but when somebody asks a question, they:
- Freeze and cannot find the right words.
- Translate from their native language.
- Speak too slowly.
- Make repeated grammar mistakes.
- Struggle with pronunciation.
- Feel embarrassed speaking with colleagues or strangers.
We should solve these problems.

After a conversation, show:

BETTER ENGLISH
