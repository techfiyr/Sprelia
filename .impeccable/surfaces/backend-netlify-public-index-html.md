## Scope and mode
Hermione marketing landing page (`backend/netlify/public/index.html`, shared `assets/site.css`). Persuade. Visitor: an adult English learner on a phone or laptop. Action: try the free lesson (the app is not published, so the working action is "Coming soon to Android" plus a visible path to the lesson preview on the page). Constraints: first-party only, no analytics, existing site tests' rules, AI disclaimer near tutors, nothing fabricated.

## Direction contract
SEED: seed:175fdce7120c1d6e (degraded roll, no challengers, no image generation, code-led)
FORM: Sing-along stage. A lyric-video / karaoke stage.
THESIS: The page proves speaking by making it visible: sentences light up word by word as if voiced, so the visitor sees the product's job in the first viewport. Refuses the category default of a headline-subcopy-button hero over a row of equal icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Drenched saturated violet stage as the ground, deeper ink-violet for pit/shadow bands, hot sun-yellow for lit words and the single primary action, coral for the speech-bubble accent, mascot in its own colours. Lyric lines set huge in a rounded heavy display face; unlit words are tinted violet-white at low opacity, lit words are solid yellow. Controls are rounded pills; captions sit in subtitle-bar bands. Recognisable with all copy removed: purple stage, yellow word-sweep, a small mascot "singer".
STORY: Visitor sees "Learn English with Hermione" light up, understands this is about speaking out loud, believes it is friendly and low-pressure (tap a line, it sings along), then learns how the lesson works, what feedback they get, that tutors are AI, and that the free lesson comes first.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The headline and subtitle as lit lyric lines on the violet stage with the mascot at stage right; a visible yellow primary action; the lit-word sweep playing once on load. Memory test answer: "a purple karaoke page where the words light up as I read them".
SIGNATURE: Tap/Enter on any practice line to replay its word-by-word light-up (first-party JS, content visible without it, reduced motion shows all lines lit and static).
RISK: A saturated dark-ish ground can read as nightlife or childish; counter with adult copy, generous spacing, strong contrast, and quiet content bands.
